Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Shanyraq is a personal tool that connects to YouTube through the YouTube Data API on behalf of its author only.

What data is accessed

With the user's permission, the app accesses the user's own YouTube channel: video metadata, uploads, playlists and comments, in order to upload videos, edit their details and read and reply to comments.

How data is stored

Access tokens and channel data are stored only on the author's own computer. Nothing is sent to third parties, sold, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Local data is deleted when the app is removed.
